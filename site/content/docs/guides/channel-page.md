---
title: "Your channel page"
description: "Details, banner, links, announcements and emotes."
---

Everything on your public page is edited in **Studio → Channel page**. Emotes have their own tab.

## Details

Channel name, stream title (shown while you are live) and the about text. The page address
cannot be changed, so old links keep working.

## Banner

PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB and 4000 pixels on a side. About 1500 by 400 works well. The file
is checked by its contents, not its name.

## Links

Up to five links with a label. Only `http` and `https` addresses are accepted, and they open in
a new tab.

## Announcements

Short posts (500 characters) shown on your channel page. Pinned posts come first. Delete one
from the same card.

## Emotes

**Studio → Emotes** lists your emotes. To add one, choose an image (PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 256
KB and 128 by 128 pixels) and a name of letters, numbers and underscores. People use it by
typing `:name:`. A channel can have up to 50. Removing an emote makes chat show its name as
plain text.

## Deleting a channel

At the bottom of the page. You must type the channel's name. It removes the channel, its
recordings, emotes, banner, chat and followers, and it cannot be undone.
