---
title: "Chat and moderation"
description: "What viewers can do in chat, and the controls owners and moderators have."
---

Every channel has one chat. It is public to read and needs an account to write in.

## For viewers

- **Reply** to a message: hover it and use the arrow. Replies show a preview of what they answer.
- **@mention** someone by typing `@username`. They get a bell notification and the message is
  highlighted for them.
- **React** with one of eight emoji. You can put up to five different ones on a message.
- **Emotes**: type `:name:` for any emote the channel owner added.
- Chat is limited to **300 characters**, and to a few messages in a burst. Sending the same text
  twice in ten seconds is dropped.
- **Anonymous mode**: under the message box, "switch to anonymous". Viewers then see
  "Anonymous" and cannot @mention you by name. Your account is still recorded, and the channel's
  owner, moderators and the server admin can see who sent a message. Change it back any time;
  earlier messages keep how they were sent.

Guests can read everything, including polls, but cannot send, react or vote.

## Pinned message and polls

The owner or a moderator can pin one message, which stays at the top of the chat. One poll can
run at a time, with two to four options. You vote once, and the result updates live and stays
visible after the poll closes.

## Moderation

Owners and moderators hover a message and open the **⋯** menu:

- **Delete message** (authors can delete their own)
- **Pin message**
- **Reveal who sent this**, for an anonymous message. Every lookup is written to the log
- **Time out 10 minutes**

In **Studio → Live** the **Chat moderation** card has:

- **Slow mode**: off, 5 seconds or 30 seconds between a viewer's messages
- **Followers only**: only people who follow the channel can write

**Studio → Moderators** lists moderators, lets the owner add one from the channel's followers
(only followers can be moderators) and shows active timeouts and bans with a **Lift** button.
Moderators cannot ban the owner or another moderator; only the owner can act on a moderator.

Owners, moderators and the server admin are not held to slow mode, followers-only or the rate
limit.

## The moderation log

Deleting someone else's message, timeouts, bans, lifting them, pinning, polls and settings
changes appear under **Recent actions** in the Studio. Reveals are logged too, but not listed
there.

## Notifications

A mention or a reply rings the bell of exactly the person involved, in either identity mode, and
an anonymous sender stays "Anonymous" in it. See your settings under **Settings → Notifications**.
