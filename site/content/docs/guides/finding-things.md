## The search box

Every page has a search box in the top bar, and the home page has a large one. Start typing and
suggestions appear under it: **channels** (with a red **Live** badge and the viewer count when
they are streaming), **videos** and **recordings**, with the part that matched in bold. Live
channels come first. Before you type, it lists the channels that are live now.

- **Up** and **Down** move through the list, **Enter** opens the highlighted row, **Escape**
  closes it.
- **Enter** with nothing highlighted, or the last row, opens the search page with every match,
  grouped by type, each group with a link to its full directory.

Search looks at channel names, handles, about texts and stream titles; video titles and
descriptions; and recording names and channel names.

## The directories

The top bar links to four directories:

| Page       | Shows                                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Live       | Channels that are streaming now, most watched first |
| Channels   | Every channel, live or not                          |
| Videos     | Videos that channel owners uploaded                 |
| Recordings | Automatic recordings of past broadcasts             |

Each has a text filter, a sort order and numbered pages (12 per page). Channels can also be
filtered to live or offline, and videos and recordings to one channel. The filters and the page
number are in the address, so you can bookmark or share any view. **Clear filters** resets them.

The home page shows the first eight of each kind with a **View all** link.

Private and suspended channels never appear in search or in any directory.
