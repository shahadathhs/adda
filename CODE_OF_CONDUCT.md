# Code of Conduct

adda is named for an informal, friendly conversation. The project's spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions) should feel like that.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Be specific and constructive in review, and accept review the same way.
- Respect that people have different experience, languages and time zones.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing someone's private
information, sexual attention, and sustained disruption of a conversation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through a GitHub private message or the
contact on the maintainer's profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may
edit or remove comments, close threads, and ban people from the project's spaces for
behaviour that breaks this code, and will explain the decision to the person concerned.

This applies in the project's spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
